How it works

Camera frames to words in milliseconds — every step on your device.

📷

01 · Capture

Mirrored webcam feed. Video is previewed instantly — tracking loads a moment later.

🔧

02 · Landmarks

MediaPipe maps up to 2 hands × 21 points, normalized for distance and position.

🤖

03 · Classify

Heuristic gates read fingers, thumb, spread and motion into letters, digits and phrases.

✅

04 · Validate

5-of-8-frame consensus plus hold-to-lock. Shaky guesses never reach your sentence.

✏️

05 · Speak

Locked signs build the sentence and speech reads it aloud the instant they lock.

🎬

06 · Celebrate

Still open palms trigger Absolute Cinema — letterbox, confetti and fanfare, no text.
